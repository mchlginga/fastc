# Purpose and Scope
-   Aims to simplify trainee registration, certificate management, and AI-based job matching for certified TESDA trainees.

## Anong gusto ma-solve ng system?
- imbis na on-site yung profiling, pag-take ng course, certification, or etc.. gawing online nalang para hindi na hustle.
- AI Job matching para ma solve yung lack of opportunities sa mga certified trainees.
